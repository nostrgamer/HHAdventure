Next up, my wife found a great product that lets you add pockets directly into the doors of the van. This makes the rear cargo doors much more usable.

![Rear cargo door pocket installed](/images/Headliner%20%2B%20Pockets%20%2B%20Fridge%20Cabinet/IMG_0099.jpg)

![Second rear cargo door pocket](/images/Headliner%20%2B%20Pockets%20%2B%20Fridge%20Cabinet/IMG_0100.jpg)

There is also a side door pocket for the lower panel, which is excellent for various kitchen extras like garbage bags.

![Side door pocket for kitchen extras](/images/Headliner%20%2B%20Pockets%20%2B%20Fridge%20Cabinet/IMG_0159.jpg)

We also found that they made another product that installs above the existing headliner. It gives us amazing space for soft items like jackets, towels, and other things that are nice to have accessible but out of the way.

![Storage above the headliner](/images/Headliner%20%2B%20Pockets%20%2B%20Fridge%20Cabinet/IMG_0160.jpg)

It even comes with a rack that can be used to hang towels.

![Headliner rack for towels](/images/Headliner%20%2B%20Pockets%20%2B%20Fridge%20Cabinet/IMG_0162.jpg)

Next up, we had received our refrigerator after taking the van camping in early May, and we wanted to make sure we had it installed before the next trip. To do that, we needed to make sure it would not move. The most secure way to do this was to secure a piece of wood through the floor of the van and then build a box on the sides that attach to the fridge.

We decided to take this a step further and build a full cabinet to the side of the fridge that would provide extra storage.

![Building the fridge cabinet](/images/Headliner%20%2B%20Pockets%20%2B%20Fridge%20Cabinet/IMG_0143.jpg)

Here you can see the rear of the fridge and the cabinet while it was being built.

![Rear of the fridge and cabinet build](/images/Headliner%20%2B%20Pockets%20%2B%20Fridge%20Cabinet/IMG_0145.jpg)

The next step was to build a door for the toilet and a drawer for our kitchen supplies, which had been in a tote under the bed for the last few trips and was very hard to access.

![Kitchen supply drawer](/images/Headliner%20%2B%20Pockets%20%2B%20Fridge%20Cabinet/IMG_0154.jpg)

![Toilet cabinet door](/images/Headliner%20%2B%20Pockets%20%2B%20Fridge%20Cabinet/IMG_0157.jpg)

My wife was the master designer behind this project and very patiently graphed out and measured everything. I was the monkey just cutting things. She also did some of the finish installation at the end that required extra patience.

![Finish installation](/images/Headliner%20%2B%20Pockets%20%2B%20Fridge%20Cabinet/IMG_0152.jpg)

The finished product looks amazing, if I do say so myself.

![Finished fridge cabinet](/images/Headliner%20%2B%20Pockets%20%2B%20Fridge%20Cabinet/IMG_0153.jpg)

This was a huge practical upgrade. The pockets give us easy-access storage, the headliner shelf adds a lot of soft storage overhead, and the fridge cabinet finally gives the refrigerator a secure home while adding usable kitchen storage next to it. We can't wait to go out camping without having to use ice and with a freezer, not to mention the convenience of having kitchen supplies at the ready.
