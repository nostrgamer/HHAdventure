---
title: "Installing the Ceiling"
date: 2026-07-17
description: "Installing the L-track, Baltic birch ceiling panels, lights, trim, and fan covers."
---

After returning from an extended Fourth of July visit with my sister, we got back to work on the van. It was finally time to finish the ceiling.

The first step was attaching L-track to the ceiling framing. The L-track would do more than cover the main front-to-back seams between the plywood panels: it would also provide strong mounting points for gear and help support our future upper cabinets.

![L-track ready for the ceiling installation](/images/Ceiling/IMG_0339.jpg)

![Installed first section of ceiling L-track](/images/Ceiling/IMG_0340.jpg)

![Jenn finishing the ceiling L-track installation](/images/Ceiling/IMG_0341.jpg)

Because the L-track would carry some of the upper cabinets' load, we also finished installing it along the upper walls.

![Upper-wall L-track installed for the future cabinets](/images/Ceiling/IMG_0344.jpg)

Next, we measured, cut, and dry-fit the main ceiling panels. We used quarter-inch Baltic birch plywood here (and we will be using this for all the van paneling), which gave us the clean wood finish we wanted without adding too much weight overhead.

![Dry-fitting the upper ceiling panels](/images/Ceiling/IMG_0347.jpg)

We cut openings for both ceiling fans, then measured and fitted the narrower plywood sections along the passenger and driver sides.

![Ceiling panel cut around a fan opening](/images/Ceiling/IMG_0348.jpg)

![Dry-fitting the passenger- and driver-side ceiling pieces](/images/Ceiling/IMG_0350.jpg)

Our plan for finishing the seams used two different materials. The L-track has a quarter-inch overhang, so it covers the main front-to-back gaps. We would use plastic trim for the remaining seams between sections of plywood. Before moving on, Jenn checked the spacing to make sure the trim would fit correctly.

![Jenn checking the spacing for the plastic trim](/images/Ceiling/IMG_0349.jpg)

With all the major pieces in place, the dry fit of the main ceiling was complete.

![Main ceiling panels fully dry-fitted](/images/Ceiling/IMG_0353.jpg)

The next job was laying out and cutting openings for the twelve LED lights that would mount directly into the plywood. The first pass with the hole saw was a little nerve-racking, but once the layout was confirmed, the rest followed.

![Cutting the first opening for an LED ceiling light](/images/Ceiling/IMG_0356.jpg)

Once every panel and opening had been cut, we took the plywood back down for finishing. We sanded each piece, cleaned away the dust, and applied several coats of clear finish.

![Sanding the ceiling plywood](/images/Ceiling/IMG_0371.jpg)

![Cleaning the plywood before clear-coating](/images/Ceiling/IMG_0370.jpg)

![Applying clear finish to the ceiling panels](/images/Ceiling/IMG_0372.jpg)

![Applying another coat of clear finish](/images/Ceiling/IMG_0373.jpg)

![Ceiling panels drying between coats](/images/Ceiling/IMG_0374.jpg)

The final coat brought out the grain of the Baltic birch and gave the panels a really nice shine.

![The finished Baltic birch ceiling panels](/images/Ceiling/IMG_0378.jpg)

Before the panels could go up permanently, the fan covers needed to be trimmed so they would fit neatly into the new ceiling.

![Trimming a fan cover to fit the ceiling](/images/Ceiling/IMG_0379.jpg)

We test-installed the trimmed covers, then removed them again and painted them black to match the rest of the ceiling hardware.

![Trimmed fan cover installed for a test fit](/images/Ceiling/IMG_0385.jpg)

![Fan covers removed and painted black](/images/Ceiling/IMG_0386.jpg)

Finally, we installed the forward-most ceiling pieces.

![Forward ceiling panels installed](/images/Ceiling/IMG_0388.jpg)

Finishing the ceiling felt like a major milestone. After months of framing, wiring, measuring, and working around everything overhead, the inside of the van suddenly looked much more finished. Better yet, we were nearly ready for one of the most exciting stages of the build: installing the upper cabinets.
