Wiring up the van was very rewarding by itself, but at that stage it mostly allowed us to continue the rest of the build. With the bed raised and insulation in, it was time to make the electrical system actually useful: mount the battery in the back of the van, mount the DC box and fuse panels, and get lights and USB charging installed.

First up was roughing in the DC box and fuses.

![Roughing in the DC box and fuse panels](/images/Electrical%20Part%202/IMG_0091.jpg)

Then I added the first circuit for the lights.

![First lighting circuit added](/images/Electrical%20Part%202/IMG_0093.jpg)

I used a dimmer switch and tested one light on the circuit first, just to make sure everything was working before wiring the rest.

![Testing the first light with the dimmer](/images/Electrical%20Part%202/IMG_0119.jpg)

Once that worked, I started adding more lights.

![Adding more ceiling lights](/images/Electrical%20Part%202/IMG_0122.jpg)

![More lights wired into the circuit](/images/Electrical%20Part%202/IMG_0123.jpg)

Finally, I put the dimmer in its temporary position with painter's tape. The walls will cover up much of the switch once everything is finished, so this should look even better later.

![Dimmer switch in its temporary position](/images/Electrical%20Part%202/IMG_0121.jpg)

Then it was time to test the lights at full power.

![Lights at full power](/images/Electrical%20Part%202/IMG_0127.jpg)

And dimmed down.

![Lights dimmed down](/images/Electrical%20Part%202/IMG_0126.jpg)

Finally, I completed a second circuit so we could have USB power in the kitchen area, which is where we tend to charge devices.

![USB power circuit for the kitchen area](/images/Electrical%20Part%202/IMG_0120.jpg)

Later on, I will wire up some individual reading lights, each with its own USB port. For now, having adjustable lights and USB power is already a huge improvement, and we are enjoying it right away.
