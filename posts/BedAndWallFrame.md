With the Transit capsules installed, we could start the next phase of the interior build: cleating. Cleating is the wood structure that the finished walls will eventually attach to, so this was one of those steps that does not look flashy at first, but it sets up everything that comes after.

![Starting the cleating layout](/images/Bed%20and%20Wall%20Frame/IMG_0017.jpg)

We started by measuring the lengthwise sections of the van and installing half-inch birch plywood along the wall areas that would eventually need finished walls.

![First section of the birch plywood](/images/Bed%20and%20Wall%20Frame/IMG_0023.jpg)

![Birch plywood cleating installed along the wall](/images/Bed%20and%20Wall%20Frame/IMG_0024.jpg)

Where possible, we used plus nut holes that were already available in the van. To line everything up, we used a hanger bolt to mark the back of the wood.

![Using the existing plus nut locations](/images/Bed%20and%20Wall%20Frame/IMG_0018.jpg)

Once the marks were transferred, we used a Forstner bit to countersink the bolts into the wood. That created a flush surface so the finished walls can eventually mount cleanly.

![Shopping for bolts](/images/Bed%20and%20Wall%20Frame/IMG_0027.jpg)

At the same time, we began raising the bed into its new position. At first we tried using milled timber for the bed supports.

![Initial bed support using milled timber shown alongside framing from our old Ikea bed](/images/Bed%20and%20Wall%20Frame/IMG_0033.jpg)

That approach did not hold up the way we wanted. The original wood was too soft, so we went back and built new bed walls from sandwiched three-quarter-inch plywood. This gave the bed the structural integrity it needed.

![Building stronger sandwiched plywood bed walls](/images/Bed%20and%20Wall%20Frame/IMG_0076.jpg)

Once completed, the bed was raised enough to create a much more useful garage underneath. The old garage height was about 21 inches. The new setup is approximately 30 inches, which gives us a lot more usable storage and should allow the future refrigerator to just fit under the bed.

![Raised bed and larger garage space](/images/Bed%20and%20Wall%20Frame/IMG_0089.jpg)

My son approved of the new work, which is always an important inspection checkpoint.

![Bed approved](/images/Bed%20and%20Wall%20Frame/IMG_0097.jpg)

This was a big practical step. The capsules gave us the sleeping width we needed, and raising the bed gave us the storage height we had been missing. Now the van is starting to feel less like a collection of individual projects and more like one connected interior build.
